## Important Notes

1. **Name Matching:** The business name on PAN, bank account, and GST must be identical. Mismatches cause ~40% of activation delays.
2. **GST Threshold:** GST registration is mandatory if annual turnover exceeds Rs 20 lakhs (Rs 10 lakhs in special category states).
3. **Board Resolution Format:** Must be on company letterhead, state the purpose (opening Razorpay payment gateway account), and be signed by at least 2 directors (excluding the authorized signatory).
4. **Timeline:** Account activation takes 2-3 business days after clean submission.
5. **Test Mode:** Development can proceed immediately with test API keys — documents are only required for live payment processing.
